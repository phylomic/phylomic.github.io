Why this is different from your sequencing vendor's bundled analysis

Sequencing vendors usually include a standard pipeline output with your data: tables of genes and p-values, produced the same way for every project. That is a useful starting point, but it is not something you can put in a paper as it stands.

  • A design and QC check before any statistics, so contamination, outliers and batch effects are caught before they become results
  • Interpretation written for a PI, which checks pathway labels against the genes behind them
  • Figures, tables and methods text ready for a manuscript
